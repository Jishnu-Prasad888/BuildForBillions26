## Before the field survey

Do not remove, plough or burn the damaged crop before the joint field survey is completed. Officials need to see the damaged field to assess the percentage of loss for each survey number.

Take clear photographs of the damaged field on the day of the damage and on the following days. Photographs should show the crop, the field boundary and, if possible, a landmark. Keep the date and time on the photographs.

## Keep these ready for the surveyor

- RTC / Pahani of the survey number
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook of the Aadhaar-seeded account
- Photographs of the damage

## If you are a tenant cultivator

If you cultivate land owned by someone else, inform the Village Accountant during the survey and carry any written lease agreement or a letter from the landowner.
