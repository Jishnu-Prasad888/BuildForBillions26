> DEMO SEED DOCUMENT. Field-by-field guide for the DEMO Crop Damage Assistance form used in this prototype. The form is a mock and is not connected to any government portal.

## Applicant name

Enter your full name exactly as it appears on your Aadhaar card or land record (RTC / Pahani). Relief payments are matched against Aadhaar, so spelling differences can delay payment.

## Father's or spouse's name

Enter the name of your father or spouse as written in your land record or Aadhaar card.

## Date of birth

Your date of birth is printed on your Aadhaar card below your name. Enter it in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

## Mobile number

Enter a 10-digit mobile number that you can receive SMS on. Preferably use the number linked with your Aadhaar so you can receive OTPs and payment updates.

## Identity document type

Choose the identity document you will provide: Aadhaar card or Driving Licence. Aadhaar is preferred because relief payments are made by Aadhaar-based Direct Benefit Transfer.

## Aadhaar number

The Aadhaar number is the 12-digit number printed on the front of your Aadhaar card and on the e-Aadhaar letter, usually in groups of four digits (for example 2345 6789 0123). An Aadhaar number never starts with 0 or 1. Do not enter the 16-digit Virtual ID.

## Driving Licence number

The Driving Licence number is printed on the front of the licence card. In Karnataka it usually starts with the state code KA followed by the RTO code, year and a serial number, for example KA05 20190012345.

## Address, district, taluk and village

Enter your current residential address. Choose the district, taluk and village where the affected land is located, as written on your RTC / Pahani.

## Survey number

The survey number (Sy. No.) identifies your land parcel. You can find it at the top of your RTC / Pahani land record, and you can download the RTC from the Karnataka Land Records (Bhoomi) portal using your district, taluk, hobli, village and survey number or name. It may include a hissa (sub-division) number, for example 45/2A.

## Extent of land

Enter the area of the affected land in acres (and guntas if applicable) as shown in the RTC / Pahani. 1 acre = 40 guntas; 1 hectare is about 2.47 acres.

## Crop, season and date of damage

Select the crop that was damaged and the season (Kharif, Rabi or Summer). Enter the date on which the damage occurred. Report the damage as early as possible; for insured crops under PMFBY, losses must be intimated within 72 hours.

## Cause of damage and estimated loss

Choose the cause, such as heavy rain / flood, drought, hailstorm or pest attack. Enter your own estimate of the percentage of crop lost. The final loss is assessed by officials through a joint survey; relief under SDRF/NDRF norms needs an assessed loss of 33% or more.

## Bank account number

Your bank account number is printed on the first page of your bank passbook and on your cheque book. Use the account that is seeded with your Aadhaar.

## IFSC code

The IFSC is an 11-character code that identifies your bank branch, for example SBIN0001234. The first four characters are letters for the bank, the fifth is always zero, and the last six identify the branch. It is printed on the passbook first page and on cheque leaves.

## Bank name

Enter the name of the bank and branch, as printed on your passbook.

## Declaration

Read the declaration and tick the box to confirm that the information you have provided is true. Providing false information may lead to rejection and recovery of any amount paid.
