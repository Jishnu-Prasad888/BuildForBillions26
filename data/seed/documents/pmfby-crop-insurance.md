## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is the Government of India crop insurance scheme. It covers farmers growing notified crops in notified areas against yield losses caused by non-preventable natural risks. Both loanee and non-loanee farmers can enrol within the seasonal enrolment deadline.

## Farmer premium

The farmer's share of premium is capped at 2% of the sum insured for Kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5% for Rabi food and oilseed crops, and 5% for annual commercial and horticultural crops. The balance premium is shared by the Central and State Governments.

## Localized calamities

Losses to individual insured fields caused by localized risks such as hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst and natural fire are assessed at the individual farm level.

## Post-harvest losses

Post-harvest losses are covered for crops kept in "cut and spread" condition in the field for drying, for a period of up to 14 days from harvesting, against specified perils such as cyclone, cyclonic rain and unseasonal rain.

## How to intimate a claim

An insured farmer should intimate the loss within 72 hours of the occurrence of the event. The loss can be reported through the Crop Insurance mobile app, the PMFBY portal, the insurance company's toll-free number, the bank branch, or the local agriculture department office. The intimation should mention the survey number, the crop, the date and cause of loss, and the approximate area affected.

## Documents usually needed

- Identity proof (Aadhaar)
- Land record (RTC / Pahani) with survey number
- Bank account details (passbook)
- Sowing certificate / declaration of the crop sown
- Insurance policy or enrolment receipt

## Claim settlement

After assessment by the insurance company and the agriculture department, the claim amount is paid directly into the farmer's bank account.
