## Kisan Credit Card

The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) provides farmers with timely credit for cultivation, post-harvest expenses and allied activities. Banks such as State Bank of India offer KCC crop loans against land records and crop details.

## Relief when a natural calamity is declared

When the State Government declares a district or area as affected by a natural calamity, banks follow the Reserve Bank of India directions on relief measures. These may include restructuring or rescheduling of existing crop loans, and sanction of fresh crop loans to affected farmers.

## Conditions

Relief measures such as restructuring are generally linked to a crop loss of 33% or more, as assessed and certified by the District / State authorities. The relief applies to borrowers in the areas declared affected by the calamity.

## What the farmer should do

1. Visit the bank branch where the crop loan / KCC account is held.
2. Carry identity proof, land record (RTC / Pahani), KCC passbook or loan account details, and any crop loss certificate or survey record issued by the authorities.
3. Request restructuring / rescheduling of the loan and, if needed, a fresh crop loan for the next season.

## Insurance link

Loanee farmers with crop loans for notified crops are often covered under PMFBY. Check with your bank whether your crop is insured and intimate losses within 72 hours.
