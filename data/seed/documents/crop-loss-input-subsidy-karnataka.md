## Overview

When crops are damaged by a notified natural calamity such as heavy rain, flood, drought or hailstorm, the State Government provides relief to affected farmers in the form of an input subsidy. The relief is paid under the norms of the State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF) and the National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF) issued by the Government of India. The State may add a top-up over these norms by a government order.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a farmer cultivating the affected land, either as the recorded owner or recorded cultivator in the land records (RTC / Pahani).
2. The crop damage must have been caused by a natural calamity notified by the State Government for that area and season, for example heavy rain, flood, drought, hailstorm or cyclone.
3. The assessed crop loss for the affected area should be 33% or more. Crop loss is assessed through a joint survey by the Revenue, Agriculture and Horticulture departments.
4. Assistance is limited to a maximum affected area per farmer as specified in the SDRF/NDRF norms in force (commonly up to 2 hectares per farmer).

## Assistance amount

The input subsidy is calculated per hectare of affected area. Different rates apply for rainfed crops, irrigated crops and perennial crops. Rates are revised periodically by the Government of India, so the amount must be checked against the current official notification. Payment is made by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the farmer.

## Required documents

- Identity proof: Aadhaar card (preferred, because payment is Aadhaar-linked) or Driving Licence.
- Land record: RTC / Pahani showing the survey number and the name of the farmer.
- Bank account details: first page of bank passbook or a cancelled cheque showing account number and IFSC.
- Evidence of crop loss: photographs of the damaged field, if available, and the joint survey / field inspection record.

## How to apply

1. Report the crop damage to the Village Accountant, the local Raitha Samparka Kendra or the Taluk office as soon as possible after the calamity.
2. Officials conduct a joint field survey and record the extent of loss for each survey number.
3. Eligible farmer details, survey numbers and bank details are entered in the State relief portal.
4. After verification, the input subsidy is credited directly to the farmer's bank account.

## Important points

- Make sure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar, otherwise the DBT payment may fail.
- Keep your RTC / Pahani updated; the survey number and extent in the application must match the land record.
- Do not pay anyone to get relief sanctioned. Relief is free of cost.
