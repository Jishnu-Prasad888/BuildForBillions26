## Overview

PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme that provides income support of ₹6,000 per year to eligible landholding farmer families. The amount is paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 every four months directly into the bank accounts of beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer.

## Eligibility

All landholding farmer families that have cultivable land recorded in their names are eligible, subject to the exclusion criteria below. A family means husband, wife and minor children.

## Exclusions

The following are not eligible: institutional landholders; farmer families where a member holds or held a constitutional post; serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government (except Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D); retired pensioners with a monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more (with the same exception); persons who paid income tax in the last assessment year; and professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects registered with professional bodies.

## eKYC and bank account

eKYC is mandatory for PM-KISAN beneficiaries. It can be done through OTP on the PM-KISAN portal, biometric authentication at a Common Service Centre, or face authentication using the mobile app. The bank account should be Aadhaar-seeded to receive instalments.

## Registration

Farmers can register through the "New Farmer Registration" option on the PM-KISAN portal, through a Common Service Centre, or through the State nodal/agriculture officer. Documents needed are Aadhaar, land ownership record and bank account details.

## Crop damage

PM-KISAN is income support; it is not a compensation for crop damage. Farmers who suffer crop loss should also check disaster relief input subsidy and crop insurance (PMFBY) claims.
