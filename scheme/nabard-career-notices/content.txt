NABARD cautions members of public not to fall prey to such spurious offers made by the unknown persons as the same are fake letters. NABARD never advises any candidate to report to any individual by name. On declaration of the results, Roll Nos. of all selected candidates are displayed on NABARD website. The candidates are advised to check details of recruitments/appointments, etc. from the Recruitment Link (Career Notices) on Bank's website, www.nabard.org.
