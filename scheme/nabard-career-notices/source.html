FAKE RECRUITMENT NOTICES AND APPOINTMENT LETTERS

We have received information that offer for jobs and/or appointment letters for various posts said to have been issued by NABARD are received by a few job aspirants. NABARD cautions members of public not to fall prey to such spurious offers made by the unknown persons as the same are fake letters. NABARD never advises any candidate to report to any individual by name. On declaration of the results, Roll Nos. of all selected candidates are displayed on NABARD website.

The candidates are advised to check details of recruitments/appointments, etc. from the Recruitment Link (Career Notices) on Bank's website, www.nabard.org. In case of any doubt, members of public may contact on following phone no. / email id.

Phone No. 022-68120083/022-26539813
Email: recruitment@nabard.org

Date: 15.02.2021
